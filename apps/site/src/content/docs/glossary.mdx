Code names in `monospace`. The guide that covers a term is linked at the end of its entry.

## The wheel

**Wheel**: the `Container` the builder returns: its rings, one event emitter, shortcuts to the main ring.

**Ring**: one spinning disc with its sections, pointers and skin. A wheel has one or more around the same centre. [Rings](/guides/rings/)

**Main ring**: the ring the wheel's shortcut methods address. `'main'` unless a config names it otherwise.

**Section**: one wedge: `id`, `label`, `value`, `weight`, `style`, `tags`, optional rich `content`. Also sector, segment. [Sections](/guides/sections/)

**Weight / arc**: a section's relative share of the ring; its arc is that share of 360 degrees. Default weight 1.

**Divider**: the line between two sections. A pointer crossing one fires `pointer:tick`.

**Start angle**: where section 0 begins, wheel-local degrees. Default -90 (twelve o'clock); sections lay out clockwise from there.

**Hub / rim**: the centre of the wheel and its outer edge. `radius(outer, inner)` sets both; `inner` is the hub radius.

**Disc / overlay**: a ring's two containers. The disc rotates (sections, labels, face); the overlay does not (bezel, hub cap, pointers).

**Dynamic sections**: weights that change at run time, animated, with labels re-centring. Cosmetic: the landing is decided by `setResult()`, never by the geometry. **Step**: one authored set of weights in a `dynamic` config. [Dynamic sections](/guides/dynamic-sections/)

## Labels

**Label**: the text a label-drawing skin writes for a section. Defaults to the id; `''` draws nothing.

**Content**: a rich label: any `Container`, or a factory that builds one from a `LabelContext` (section, radii, slot, a bound `fit()`). [Sections](/guides/sections/#rich-labels)

**Slot**: the box a section offers a label at its label radius: the chord across the wedge and the radial room around it. `labelSlot()` computes it.

**Fit mode**: how content meets its slot: `'contain'` (default), `'cover'`, `'width'`, `'height'`, `'none'`. Helpers `scaleToFit`, `fitContainer`, `fitText`.

**Label orientation**: `'radial'` (hub to rim), `'tangential'` (along the arc, top toward the rim), `'tangential-in'` (top toward the hub, for wheels read from six o'clock), `'upright'` (stays upright as the disc turns).

## Spinning

**Profile**: a `SpinProfile`: cruise speed, wind-up, stop duration, ease, turn range. Only `spinSpeed` is required. Registered by name, switched with `setSpeed()`. Presets: `NORMAL`, `TURBO`, `CINEMATIC`, `QUICK`. [Profiles](/docs/api-profiles/)

**Wind-up / cruise**: the acceleration to `spinSpeed`, then the constant-speed phase while the result is awaited. `spin:cruise` marks the hand-over.

**Planned stop**: the list of legs computed when the stop may begin. One deceleration whose ease starts at the cruise speed, plus any tease and settle legs; playback is deterministic. [Spin lifecycle](/guides/spin-lifecycle/)

**Leg**: one piece of a plan: a distance, a duration, an ease. Kinds: `decel`, `creep`, `hesitate`, `push`, `dwell`, `skip`, `settle`, `bounce`, `bounce-return`. No plan has a reverse leg before the rest.

**Turns**: full rotations in the stop. The planner picks the count in `minTurns..maxTurns` whose duration is closest to `stopDuration`.

**Ease**: the curve a leg follows: a GSAP-style name (`power3.out`, `sine.inOut`) or a function. The stop ease must be an ease-out.

**Idle**: a slow rotation while nothing happens. `wheel.idle.start()` / `stop()`, `ring.idle.isActive`; `spin()` takes over and idle resumes after. [Idle](/guides/idle/)

**Result timeout**: `resultTimeoutMs` on `spin()`: reject the promise and land on the current section when no result arrives in time.

## Landing

**Target**: a `WheelTarget`: `{ section }`, `{ index }`, `{ value }`, `{ angle }` or `{ position }`, the first three with an optional `offset`. [Adapters](/guides/adapters/)

**Landing angle**: the wheel-local angle that ends under the pointer. Fixed at `setResult()`; skips, slams and weight changes never move it. [Landing](/guides/landing/)

**Landing mode**: where inside the section: `'center'`, `'random'` (kept `margin` off the dividers), or `'exact'` (the offset or angle you passed).

**Settle**: the move after landing: `'none'`, `'center'` (glide to the middle after `delayMs`) or `'bounce'` (overshoot `bounceDeg`, spring back).

**Skip**: `skip()`: a fast-forward to the landing over the profile's `skipDuration`, at least a third of a turn. Throws before the result is in; `requestSkip()` holds the press until it is allowed. **Slam**: `slamStop()` snaps to the final position synchronously.

**Anticipation / near-miss**: a planned tease: the pointer heads for the bait, lands on the target, rests by the line the two share. The wheel comes to rest once, on the result. [Anticipation](/guides/anticipation/)

**Bait**: the section the player is meant to hope for. Must neighbour the landing in the spin direction, else the tease is dropped with a warning.

**Creep / stutter / stall**: the three styles. Creep crawls through the bait and barely crosses the line; stutter all but stalls short of the line, then slips over; stall enters the target and dies just short of the bait's line. `'auto'` picks by geometry.

**Rest**: where the pointer ends after a tease, as a fraction of the target's arc from the shared divider (0.22 creep and stutter, 0.15 stall). `'keep'` leaves the landing mode in charge.

**Protected skip**: `protectSkip`: the first press fast-forwards to the start of the crawl and lets the tease play; the second lands. `skip:requested` reports `protectedByAnticipation`.

## The pointer

**Pointer**: the fixed mark a ring is read against. Also tongue, flapper, stopper, needle. Results are read against the first one. [Pointers](/guides/pointers/)

**Pin / tip**: the point the pointer pivots around, and its far end. A skin draws with the pin at its origin and the tip at `+length` on x. `tipInset` is how far the tip reaches past the rim.

**Facing**: `'inward'` sits on the rim with the tip toward the hub; `'outward'` sits at the hub pointing out.

**Peg**: a small circle on the disc the tongue touches, one per divider by default. `pegs.inset` defaults to `tipInset + size - 2`, a 2 px bite into the blade. [The tongue](/recipes/tongue/)

**Blade / flap**: the tongue as the contact model sees it, a triangle hinged at the pin; the flap is its deflection: the smallest swing from where it stands that keeps the peg outside the blade, sprung back by `stiffness` and `damping`, never past `maxAngle`. `elasticity` and `friction` pad the peg.

**Reach**: whether the peg ring runs between a pointer's pin and tip. A pointer out of reach warns once and stays still.

**Drag**: `flap.drag`: how hard the tongue holds the ring back while a peg climbs it. Drawn only: `ring.visualRotationDeg` moves, `rotationDeg` never does, and it relaxes to zero at rest.

**Tick**: a divider passing under a pointer. `pointer:tick` carries the pointer, the sections either side, the speed and the direction.

## Skins and art

**Skin**: what a ring looks like. The ring owns motion; the skin owns pixels. `graphics` (default), `debug`, `texture`, `spine`, `headless`, or your own `RingSkin`. [Skins](/guides/skins/)

**Skin config**: the serialisable form, `{ type: 'graphics', ... }`. Instances are recorded as `{ type: 'custom' }` by `toConfig()`.

**Asset resolver**: `{ texture(key) }`, what `fromConfig()` uses to turn texture keys into loaded textures.

**Face**: the painted disc as one texture. **Plate**: one wedge as its own sprite, authored upright with its outward edge at the top. **Bezel**: the fixed frame round the rim, on the overlay. [The disc](/recipes/disc-art/)

**Decoration**: a per-section sprite in `TextureRingSkin`, placed from the section's `midAngle` and kept there when weights move.

**Bone**: in `SpineRingSkin`, the skeleton bone the engine rotates. The rest of the skeleton stays fixed and keeps animating.

**Highlight**: a skin's emphasis on one section. `wheel.highlight(id)` / `highlight(null)`.

## Angles

**Clockwise positive**: degrees increase clockwise on screen, as in PixiJS.

**Local angle**: an angle on the disc, rotating with it. Screen angle = local angle + rotation, so the pointer at screen angle `p` reads local `p - rotation`. A clockwise spin sweeps decreasing local angles under the pointer.

**Entry / exit edge**: the divider a pointer meets first and last for a spin direction. Clockwise, the entry edge is the section's `endAngle`.

**Normalised angle**: folded into 0..360 with `normalizeDeg`. Sections keep unnormalised, increasing angles so section i's `endAngle` equals section i+1's `startAngle`.

## Tooling

**Config**: `WheelConfig`, the JSON twin of a builder: `toConfig()` / `fromConfig()`, checked by `assertWheelConfig`. **Template**: a ready-made config from `WheelTemplates`. [Configs](/guides/configs/)

**Adapter**: `createTargetAdapter(config)`: turns a server response into a `WheelTarget`. [Adapters](/guides/adapters/)

**Ticker**: the PixiJS `Ticker` the wheel advances on. Everything moves by `deltaMS`, so `FakeTicker` makes spins deterministic. [Testing](/guides/testing/)

**Harness**: `createTestWheel` and friends: a headless wheel, seeded randomness, `expectPointerOn`, `captureEvents`.

**Notice**: a console warning with a stable code (`bait-too-far`, `ease-slope`, `landing-moved`) for a legal but suspicious call. `setLogLevel()` silences them.

**Debug overlay / snapshot / arc**: `debugOverlay()` draws the geometry over the wheel, `debugSnapshot()` is the state as JSON, `debugArc()` the ring as a text strip. `enableDebug()` puts all three on `window.__PIXI_WHEELS_DEBUG`. [Debugging](/guides/debugging/)
