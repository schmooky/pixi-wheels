title: Glossary
order: 5
eyebrow: Reference
description: The words this library uses, in one place.
---

**Anticipation**: a planned near-miss. The pointer heads for the *bait* and lands on the *target*. Styles: creep, stutter, overshoot.

**Arc**: a section's angular size in degrees, its weight's share of 360.

**Bait**: the section the player is meant to hope for during a tease.

**Cruise**: the constant-speed phase between the wind-up and the stop, while the result is awaited.

**Disc**: the rotating container of a ring. **Overlay**: the fixed one.

**Dynamic sections**: weights that change at run time, animated, with labels re-centring. Cosmetic.

**Entry edge**: the boundary of a section a pointer meets first for a given spin direction. Clockwise, that is the section's `endAngle`.

**Facing**: `inward` seats a pointer on the rim with the tip toward the hub; `outward` seats it at the hub pointing out.

**Flap**: the pointer's spring deflection on each divider crossing.

**Landing angle**: the wheel-local angle that ends under the pointer. Fixed when the result arrives.

**Leg**: one piece of a planned stop: a distance, a duration, an ease, forwards or reverse.

**Local angle**: an angle on the disc, rotating with it. Screen angle = local + rotation.

**Pointer**: the fixed mark a ring is read against. Also tongue, flapper, stopper.

**Profile**: a `SpinProfile`, the timing of a spin. Registered by name; switched with `setSpeed`.

**Ring**: one spinning disc with its sections, pointers and skin. A wheel has one or more.

**Section**: one wedge: id, label, value, weight, style. Also sector.

**Settle**: the move after landing: a glide to the centre or a bounce.

**Skin**: what a ring looks like. Graphics, debug, texture, Spine, or your own.

**Slam**: `slamStop()`: snap to the final position now. **Skip**: a short fast-forward to the landing.

**Target**: a `WheelTarget`: where a spin lands, as the server decides it.

**Weight**: a section's relative arc share.
