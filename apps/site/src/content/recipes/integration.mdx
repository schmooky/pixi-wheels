## Sound hooks

Every audio cue as an event listener, on a real audio engine: [`@schmooky/zvuk`](https://zvuk.schmooky.dev) plays the Super Wheel's own sounds (Playson, used with permission): the activation hit, the spin, the anticipation riser, the landing and one win sting per prize tier. The tick volume and pitch follow the ring speed. Sound needs a click: press Spin.

## Debug view

The plain skin plus the live overlay: dividers with angles, the pointer from pin to tip, the pegs, the planned landing in yellow, a HUD with state, speed and the current leg. `debugArc()` prints the same as text in the console. Configs that round-trip through JSON are on the [starters page](/recipes/starters/#from-a-template-and-back-to-json).
