## Sound hooks

Every audio cue as an event listener, on a real audio engine: [`@schmooky/zvuk`](https://zvuk.schmooky.dev) plays the Super Wheel's own sounds (Playson, used with permission): the activation hit, the spin, the anticipation riser, the landing and one win sting per prize tier. The tick volume and pitch follow the ring speed. Sound needs a click: press Spin.
