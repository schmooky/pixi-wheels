The wheel knows where it will land the moment the result arrives. Anticipation spends that knowledge on the player: the pointer heads for the jackpot, slows on it, and does not quite get there. Whatever the style, it comes to rest on the result right next to the line it almost crossed.

Every style is a set of planned legs. The bait must be a neighbour of the landing in the spin direction; the engine checks and warns rather than faking a tease that would not read.

## Creep

Decelerate to a crawl exactly as the pointer enters the bait, keep slowing across it, barely cross the line into the result and stop there.

## Stutter

Stop with the pointer inside the jackpot, hold while everyone leans in, nudge over the line.

## Overshoot and roll back

The bait sits just after the landing: the wheel runs out of momentum with the pointer a few degrees past the line, into the jackpot, holds a beat, and rolls softly back onto the result. "It was going to be GRAND." No options set; the defaults are the beat.

<RecipeDemo code="bait-overshoot" />

## Auto style

`'auto'` (the default) reads the geometry: bait before the landing creeps, bait after it overshoots, bait far away is dropped with a console warning.
